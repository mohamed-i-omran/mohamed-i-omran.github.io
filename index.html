About

Technology audit grounded in the full population of data

I'm an IT Auditor at BDO Egypt with 3+ years across IT audit and data analytics, including 2.5+ years delivering ITGC, ITAC and RCM engagements for banking and enterprise clients against SOX, COBIT, ISO 27001 and NIST.

I apply audit data analytics to full transaction populations, extracting and testing journal data in CaseWare IDEA for journal entry testing, completeness and accuracy validation, and exception identification.

My work extends into business process controls, segregation of duties and delegation of authority reviews, and I turn findings into risk-rated, root-cause-based recommendations for stakeholders.
